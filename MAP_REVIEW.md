## Location semantics and interaction

The region filter uses each profile’s curriculum region, never an inference from its latitude. National strategy can therefore have a physical reference in the north without being reclassified as a north-only event.

- Giơnevơ, Paris 1969 and Paris 1973 remain overseas list entries; no domestic marker is fabricated.
- Broad northern recovery programmes, southern movements and national policy records retain an area label with no arbitrary single point.
- Tết Mậu Thân uses Huế and Sài Gòn as representative locations, explicitly not the full campaign footprint. The 1972 offensive uses Quảng Trị as one representative direction.
- The 1972 air campaign has both Hà Nội and Hải Phòng references. Multiple markers can refer to the same profile; list totals count unique event IDs.
- Clicking a city filters the chronological list; selecting an event shows its date, context, decision, result and sources. Enter/Space work on markers, with visible focus and equivalent list controls.
- Reading a map preview does not award points or quiz mastery. Period/all/view preferences are validated and restored without resetting existing scores or game sessions.

Decorative arrows were removed from navigational labels, card corners, photo/source links and relation headings. Ordering movement arrows still perform real movement; the map north indicator shows an actual bearing.
