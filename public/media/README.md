# Historical media

Five local photographs are integrated: Tours 1920, Ba Đình 1945, Điện Biên Phủ 1954, Đường Kách mệnh (artifact photographed in 2017), and Paris 1973. Full credits, source URLs, exact downloaded-file SHA-256 hashes, check dates and source reuse declarations are in [provenance.json](provenance.json); captions and links also appear in the app.

The book photograph is CC BY-SA 4.0 by Hoangkid: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The other files retain the exact public-domain designation declared on their source pages; the PD-Vietnam territory is explicit. Commons-generated thumbnails are used without additional cropping, colorization or retouching. Original files are linked separately.

The TTXVN tank photograph is a source lead only, with no local file or assumed open license. See ../../SOURCE_REVIEW.md for the review method and caption limitations.
