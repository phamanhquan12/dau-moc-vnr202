# Historical media

Eight local photographs are integrated: Tours 1920, Ba Đình 1945, Điện Biên Phủ 1954, Đường Kách mệnh (artifact photographed in 2017), Paris 1973, Giơnevơ 1954, the Nguyễn Thị Định memorial (photographed 2008) and the National Assembly building (photographed 2025). Full credits, source URLs, exact downloaded-file SHA-256 hashes, check dates and source reuse declarations are in [provenance.json](provenance.json); captions and links also appear in the app.

The book photograph is CC BY-SA 4.0 by Hoangkid: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. The Nguyễn Thị Định statue photograph is CC BY-SA 3.0 by Bùi Thụy Đào Nguyên, and the National Assembly building photograph is CC BY 2.0 by David Stanley. Their source and license links are recorded in provenance.json and the application. The other files retain the exact public-domain designation declared on their source pages; the PD-Vietnam territory is explicit. Commons-generated thumbnails are used without additional cropping, colorization or retouching. Original files are linked separately.

The TTXVN tank photograph is a source lead only, with no local file or assumed open license. See ../../SOURCE_REVIEW.md for the review method and caption limitations.
