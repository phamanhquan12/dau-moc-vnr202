## Supplementary grounding and distinctions

- WTO: [official member information](https://www.wto.org/english/theWTO_e/countries_e/vietnam_e.htm) establishes **11 January 2007** as effective membership. Textbook pages 165–166 describe the admission process with a November 2006 milestone. The WTO profile and its quiz explanations use the official membership record first, with the textbook supplied for policy context. No quiz answer year changed.
- State unification: [Báo Điện tử Chính phủ, Quốc hội khóa VI](https://baochinhphu.vn/vai-net-ve-quoc-hoi-nuoc-chxhcn-viet-nam-10271664.htm) corroborates **2 July 1976** for the naming decision. The textbook gives the broader first-session period. An initially found Quốc hội catalogue URL returned an unreadable response locally, so the working government article is used in the app.
- Marine economy: [Resolution 36-NQ/TW, 22 October 2018](https://tulieuvankien.dangcongsan.vn/van-kien-tu-lieu-ve-dang/hoi-nghi-bch-trung-uong/khoa-xii/nghi-quyet-so-36-nqtw-ngay-22102018-hoi-nghi-lan-thu-tam-ban-chap-hanh-trung-uong-dang-khoa-xii-ve-chien-luoc-phat-trien-ben-3453), Part II, supports the sustainable-development, environment and national-defense context within chapter 3.
- Founding of the Party: textbook printed page 27 distinguishes the conference period from the commemorative date 3 February. That distinction is visible beside the source.
- Tết Mậu Thân: include printed page 106's assessment of losses and limitations as well as strategic impact.
- The December 1972 campaign is situated within military, political and diplomatic developments; chronological order alone is not presented as proof of a single cause.
